# Statistics

Open the Statistics page from the Nomas Motion menu-bar icon:

1. Click the Nomas Motion menu-bar icon.
2. Choose **Statistics...**.

You can also choose **Settings...** and then select the **Statistics** tab.

Statistics collection is enabled by default. By default, a record is materialized
after five minutes of threshold-crossing motion, with a short prelude retained
for context. This is a trip heuristic, not proof that the Mac is in a vehicle;
persistent desk vibration can produce a false positive. This data stays on the
Mac and is not sent to any server. It only leaves the machine if the user
chooses to send a false-positive bug report.
